# Pizza-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard using Excel to analyze sales, orders, revenue, and customer trends.

## Key Insights

Analyzed Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Pizzas Sold, and Average Order Value to evaluate overall sales performance.
Identified daily and monthly sales trends to understand customer demand patterns.
Analyzed sales performance across different pizza categories to identify high-performing categories.
Compared pizza sizes to understand customer size preferences.
Identified top-selling pizzas based on sales and quantity.
Analyzed order distribution by day and month to identify periods with higher customer demand.
Used Excel dashboard visualizations to convert raw sales data into clear and actionable business insights.
